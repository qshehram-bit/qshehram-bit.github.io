A letter I wanted you to keep

Happy Birthday meri pyari si Rammo bchaaa. Aaj 29 September hai aur honestly aaj ka din mere liye bohot special hai because aaj meri best friend duniya mein ayi thi. Best friend kabhi kabhi ek chota sa word lagta hai, lekin tumhare liye mere liye is word ke andar bohot saari memories, care, pagalpan aur woh saari choti choti cheezein hain jo sirf hum dono samajhte hain. Tum meri favourite notification ho, woh naam jiska message screen par aate hi automatically smile aa jati hai. Tumhari random baatein, tumhara bachpana, tumhari nonstop hasi, mujey tang karna, random nakhre aur phir khud hi bchaaa ban jana — honestly ye sab tumhari woh qualities hain jin ki wajah se tum meri favourite person to talk to ban gayi.



Mujey sabse zyada khushi is baat ki hai ke hamara bond sirf achay moments tak limited nahi raha. Humne boring days bhi dekhe, stupid arguments bhi kiye, random plans bhi banaye, endless gossip bhi ki, aur aisi baatein bhi ki jin ka koi sense nahi tha lekin phir bhi hum hans hans ke pagal ho jate the. Tumne meri bohot si baatein suni hain, mujey motivate kiya hai, meri bakwas tolerate ki hai aur jab mujey kisi ki zarurat hoti thi tab genuinely ek best friend ki tarah mere sath rahi ho. Ye cheezein mere liye kabhi normal nahi hongi.



Mujey tumhari wohi real wali personality sabse zyada achi lagti hai — tumhara childish side, tumhari pagal hasi, tumhara emotional hona, tumhara random serious ho jana, cats ke sath tumhara pagalpan, aur woh choti choti habits jinhe shayad tum khud notice bhi nahi karti. Yehi sab milkar meri Rammo bchaaa banti hai. Aur honestly mujey tumhari perfection nahi chahiye, mujey meri wahi pagal best friend chahiye jo kabhi mujey gawar bolti hai, kabhi tang karti hai aur kabhi bina reason ke hansa deti hai.



Aaj tumhara birthday hai to meri sabse pehli dua ye hai ke Allah tumhe hamesha khush rakhe, tumhe sukoon de, tumhare dreams complete kare, tumhe success de aur tumhari life se unnecessary tension door kare. Allah tumhari smile hamesha safe rakhe aur tumhe har woh opportunity de jo tum deserve karti ho. InshaAllah tumhari life ka ye new year tumhare liye bohot beautiful memories lekar aaye.



Aur agar kabhi tum bhool jao ke tumhari life mein tumhari care karne wala ek pagal best friend bhi hai, to is page par wapas aa jana. Yahan tumhe tumhari pictures, hamari memories aur tumhara woh Buddy milega jo tumhari choti choti khushiyon ko genuinely celebrate karta hai. Choosing you as my best friend is one of the things I will always be grateful for, bchaaa.



So today no overthinking, no sadness, no unnecessary tension. Aaj sirf enjoy karna hai, cake khana hai, pictures leni hain, hasna hai aur apna birthday properly celebrate karna hai. Happy Birthday meri pyari si Rammo, meri bchaaa, meri best friend aur meri favourite notification. Allah tumhe hamesha apni protection mein rakhe. Ab jao cake cut karo aur biggest piece Buddy ke liye bachana, warna Buddy naraz hojayega.
